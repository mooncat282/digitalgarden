## Overview

- [stated] Official name "Fantasy House (and where to find them)" (the "and where to find them" part was a joke); nickname "phoenix AU", a quick call to tell it apart from other AUs. Named after Meo finished watching Game of Thrones.
- [stated] Called both the first AU for this story and the first AU overall, and not even for Kyu. Sentimental, not considered her best story, but wholesome (some lives toxic).
- [stated] Made for romance (and sex); mostly no plot, the characters just being themselves and having fun. Big events stay in the background, "like news you read in a newspaper". Side characters sometimes get small subplots.
- [stated] Effectively many smaller AUs in one: medieval politics with wars between houses and races, a Middle East royalty aesthetic, a French-revolution-style life (prison, revolution, side quests), a lady-and-maid love story. One lifetime of W is still undecided because no interesting history fit.
- [stated] Eras are picked mainly for aesthetic, not exact history, but they still loosely follow it.

## Premise

- [stated] Isekai. An ancient phoenix, bored of eternal life, kidnapped a human soul from another world to be its avatar and lives inside them, watching their life like a kdrama. It chose an avatar with fire, not a peaceful soul, for entertainment.
- [stated] The phoenix is a representation of reincarnation (birth, death, burn, destruction, the cycle) and luck, meaning the will of the universe. The most powerful still-active being in that world; Meo compares it to Shiva.
- [stated] The avatar, mr.K (also "Ser K"), asked for his soulmate W to be brought too. Only mr.K got the phoenix's eternal life; W has a human soul, so W is born, lives a full human life, dies, and reincarnates with no memories. Mr.K finds W again every time and starts over. In some lives he tells W everything, in some he does not.
- [stated] The couple's name is "kiwi". W's gender varies by life.
- [stated] Every phoenix reburns now and then, not just mr.K.

- [stated] The phoenix's luck makes them extremely good at business and financial matters. Money is the best proof of this luck.

## Mr.K

- [stated] Silky red hair (bird feather mixed with flame), very handsome, friendly, approachable face. A soul in two bodies merged, so he can switch between male and female at will; not a phoenix power, but the phoenix made it possible. That is why kiwi can have children in almost every life, whatever gender W is.
- [stated] Normal people think mr.K is the phoenix and call him "the great phoenix", partly because his eldest son Dustin is called the phoenix. Everyone knows the great phoenix is genderfluid.
- [stated] Early on he was fiery and behaved like a human with power he did not yet understand: fought in many wars (hence "Ser"), intentionally burned villages, played a big role in human politics to marry W. He grew more peaceful over time as W reincarnated again and again.
- [stated] Outside of always finding W, Mr.K is not predictable at all.
- [stated] Does a "reburn" now and then (burn and return to normal, not a full regression) to balance energy, since he had not fully controlled the phoenix force.
- [stated] Reburns as an infant at times, and then is the most chaotic child in the neighborhood, too small and cute for anyone to stop him. He times these reburns to match W's reincarnation so they grow up the same age, and he keeps away from W during the reburn because the energy blast is dangerous. Growing from baby bird to full adult phoenix takes the same number of years as a human.
- [stated] Uses his luck to push W's, so W is almost never born poor or average. A good, loving parent, and he never forced W to have children if W did not want them (a toxic life exists where he flatly refused).
- [stated] Female form has a yearly egg, fertilized or not; mr.K simply stays male while waiting for W's reincarnation to avoid it. Each daughter handles the yearly egg her own way.
- [stated] Once a diplomat, he still does not come across as peaceful. Diplomacy, not his style. Dustin asked him to lift the elf banishment; mr.K agreed to be present so the great phoenix's consent was clear.
- [stated] Tried to "renovate" the dragon population in his peaceful era; they kept disappearing when he looked away, and some survive today somewhere safe that humans do not know about. Meo imagines he carried a baby dragon to a hidden island, but this is not fixed, and how he did it is unknown.
- [stated] Political stance: because of the extreme imbalance their luck would cause in politics, he tells the children to try not to interfere too much. After the first W life he didn't join politics much anymore; he just chills around like a peaceful Buddha.
- [stated] He does not concern himself with what his children do, good or evil. If a child does evil, someone else in the house with the opposite opinion will step in to stop them.
- Power Level: Nothing is more powerful than him in that world. He's literally karma/luck itself, a former warrior-strategist hand-picked by the phoenix for entertainment. If he enters politics, he dominates.
- The Trolling: He uses his luck to send endless isekai adventure parties to the Tiefling King's doorstep, pointing them in the right direction just to watch the King scream at him.
- The "Normal Guy" Act: He says "I'm just a normal guy 🥺" while being a cosmic final boss. He lets people forget how terrifying he is.
- The Prayer Hotline: Other races literally pray to him as a god you can meet in this world.
